IDs are nonempty strings; entityId is an opaque identifier of at most 64 characters with no dot, slash or backslash, and never `__proto__`, `prototype` or `constructor`. Use simple IDs such as exp-1 and skill-1; reuse one entityId only for fields of the same entry.
