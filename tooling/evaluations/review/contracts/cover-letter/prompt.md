# Benchmark annotation clarification

Keep the normal length at 180–320 whitespace-separated words in three or four paragraphs. Count the body before returning it. Arabic clitics remain attached. Expand only with supplied facts and honest motivation. Never invent experience to reach the minimum.

Open with the supplied role and employer. If the opening links candidate experience to the role, copy that full factual sentence into claims and cite every fact it uses. The same annotation rule applies to a closing sentence that mentions prior experience. A neutral invitation to talk needs no claim.

A citation must support every candidate assertion in its sentence, including any title and dates. Add the title's CV ID even when another cited fact describes that role's work.

A job-duty bridge describes relevance, not a past duty. Give it separate jobSpanIds for the advertised duties and evidenceIds for the actual CV experience. Do not imply that the candidate performed those advertised duties.
