The male photo page explicitly identifies the free license above. Both portraits are cropped to square head-and-shoulders images, resized to 512×512 and compressed locally as JPEGs. The owner-provided female JPEG is 29,621 bytes, within the male JPEG's 36,084-byte budget; a 16,600-byte WebP compatibility copy replaces the former stock photo for already-saved sample CVs. No remote image is hotlinked. Do not redistribute the male photograph as a stock-photo collection, imply endorsement, sell it unmodified, or depict its model in an offensive or misleading context. Generated template thumbnails combine these portraits with clearly fictional example CV content.

Regenerate previews through the existing `/templates/<template>.pdf` production preview route with `node tooling/templates/generate-thumbnails.mjs --url http://localhost:3030`. The wrapper renders all 18 templates in Arabic and English through the unchanged PDF renderer and rasterizes their first pages. Each template has a stable alternating male/female sample, with the same persona in both languages. Arabic files remain at `pdf/<template>.pdf`, `jpg/<template>.jpg` and `thumb/<template>.webp`; English files use the corresponding `en-US/` subdirectory. All three formats are regenerated together.
