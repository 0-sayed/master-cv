---
title: "Downloading your CV"
description: "Download the actual selected PDF for free, including when review findings remain."
---

Choose **Download PDF** in the editor. On desktop, **More download formats** opens the export panel, where you can choose a file name. PDF is available; Word is visibly unavailable pending Arabic and English document-integrity approval.

![Arabic mobile export sheet with PDF available and Word unavailable](/images/guides/t004/export-ar-mobile.png)

The selected browser PDF artifact is shared by preview, checking and download. After an edit, a new artifact and new results are required. Temporary history or template previews cannot replace the current artifact. Download preparation, error and browser handoff are distinct states; a failed generation can be retried.

Review findings and incomplete checks do not block free PDF download. Resolve a save conflict before downloading so that the chosen copy is explicit. A browser download starting does not prove that the operating system saved the file.

Signed server download URLs bind the owned CV's saved revision and expire after ten minutes. A subsequent edit makes an older URL stale. Server artifacts are separately generated and have their own byte hashes; they are not interchangeable with browser artifacts.
