---
title: "The CV editor"
description: "Write, design, review and download a saved CV with truthful save and recovery states."
---

**Write** edits this CV's information. **Design** changes its template and document styling. **Check** separates PDF readability, writing advice and evidence for a selected job. On a phone, **Page** opens the preview.

![English desktop editor with a synthetic CV and its current font warning](/images/guides/t004/write-en-desktop.png)

Edits belong to this CV. They do not update your career information or another language variant automatically. Changing the interface language keeps document content intact.

The save indicator distinguishes saving, acknowledged saving, offline changes, failure and conflict. Local recovery is offered only when the browser stored a valid draft for this account and document. Restore or discard it explicitly after reopening; editing waits until you choose, preserving the recovery draft.

When another session has saved a newer version, saving stops and both sets of edits remain available. Choose **Save my edits as a new copy** or **Open saved copy**. Neither choice silently overwrites the other version. History and download wait until a conflict is resolved.

Preview, checking and browser PDF download use the same captured document artifact. An edit invalidates its results; an account or document switch discards obsolete work. Fit waits for a matching render and reports cancellation or timeout without claiming success.
