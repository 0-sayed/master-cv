---
title: "Reviewing your CV"
description: "Read separate PDF and writing results without a hiring score."
---

Open **Check** in the editor. The review shows three independent areas:

![English mobile check with independent readability findings for a minimal synthetic CV](/images/guides/t004/check-en-mobile.png)

- **PDF readability** checks the actual selected PDF bytes, including recovered text and reading order. Findings include the available evidence and page reference.
- **Writing and content** gives deterministic advice about clarity and missing information. Advice does not predict hiring acceptance.
- **Evidence for a selected job** remains **Not checked** until job comparison is available.

Results can be checking, complete, partial, stale, skipped or failed. Partial coverage does not certify unread pages or skipped checks. Arabic extraction can differ between hiring systems. After changing the CV, use a current result before drawing conclusions.

A deterministic fix opens a before-and-after preview. **Accept change** changes only this CV; **Keep as it is** leaves it unchanged. A preview captured before a later edit cannot be applied. AI writing suggestions remain unavailable pending the required consent and processing work.

PDF download remains free and available with findings, partial coverage or a failed check. A check is not permission to modify your facts and is not a guarantee of acceptance.
