# Personal career benchmark: lessons from tasks-v1

2026-11-17. This is the owner’s one-page account, followed by an evidence appendix.

## What happened and what we learned

The campaign compared Luna none, Luna low, and Terra medium across six career tasks in Arabic, English, and mixed text. DeepSeek judged whether answers stayed faithful to the supplied facts. The final matrix contains 1,944 sessions and 648 translation ratings from two independent Astra AI reviews. No human review or release approval follows from this study.

The work began with an audit and repeated Arabic reviews. The audit found an unreachable confidence requirement, duplicated test cases, weak examples, and gaps in spending controls. Those problems needed repairs before paid measurement. A holdout is a case reserved for final measurement. Repairing rules after reading holdouts weakens that measurement, even when the repair corrects a real defect.

Early paid runs stopped because the judge rejected correct control answers. A control answer has a known expected result. The team clarified relevance and missing-evidence wording, then changed the allowed false-reject count from zero to one and finally three among 18 honest controls. All valid planted defects still needed detection. Small smoke samples also caused overly broad model exclusions. The repair kept all finalists in smoke and judged eligibility separately for each task.

Rejection voting added two independent judgments after an initial rejection. It exposed more broken judge responses. Replies ended early, DeepSeek produced long whitespace tails, and repeated IDs invalidated otherwise usable judgments. Larger reply allowances, whitespace stops, identical-entry removal, and bounded retries addressed different causes. Faulty replies became abstentions, never accepting votes. More tokens alone did not solve the problem.

Cost reports also mixed temporary spending reservations with actual charges. Serial requests made full runs take many hours. The team separated charges from reservations and introduced bounded parallel requests. Resume then exposed an unknown-billing threshold, unsettled holds, and negative settlement arithmetic during an internet outage. Repairs retained paid answers and settled receipts before new requests. The OpenRouter key’s total limit formed another spending barrier beyond the account balance.

After full inference, 11 translation sessions remained incomplete and letters lacked full evidence. Diagnosis also found hand-written meaning rules rejecting supported summaries, job evidence, interview premises, and letters. These were shared production bugs. Offline repair recovered 329 safety acceptances without replacing writer answers. Scoped continuation completed translation and added 324 letters.

The final decision provisionally selects Terra medium for tailoring and interview preparation. Summary, job evidence, translation, and letters remain unselected. Safety acceptance does not prove useful writing. Prior holdout inspection prevents a claim about unseen cases.

For next time, freeze the rules before final measurement. Keep code rules focused on format, evidence boundaries, and explicit safety constraints. Use an entailment judge, which tests whether source facts support meaning, for paraphrases. Replay saved receipts and simulated failures offline before every paid run. Treat provider glitches as retryable within fixed limits. Pilot promptfoo for request dispatch and reporting before expanding custom machinery. Budget the account, each key, and each payer separately.

## Appendix: ordered stops, causes, repairs, and evidence

The numbered sequence follows the campaign. Some operational failures recurred during the final campaign. Historical commands and limits in linked reports describe their own revision.

1. Preparation and Arabic review, November 16. The audit found duplicated letter holdouts and a confidence requirement that the sample size made unreachable. Arabic reviews found unnatural or unsupported examples. Repairs replaced duplicates, improved examples, and required matching approval records. The owner adopted provisional selection. Lesson: prove the study can answer its question before paying. See the [audit](personal-career-benchmark-history.md#2026-11-16-benchmark-audit-dispositions) and [Arabic review history](personal-career-benchmark-arabic-review.md).

2. First paid calibration stops, 12:24 and 14:15. The judge rejected honest answers, including a relevance bridge to advertised duties. That bridge did not claim past experience. The repair clarified the judge instructions and identifier inventory. Lesson: measure the judge with honest answers and planted inventions before measuring writers. See the [verifier repair](personal-career-benchmark-history.md#bridge-policy) and [canary diagnosis](../../master-cv-design-archive/ui-audit/final-t008-canary-diagnose.md).

3. Smoke stop, 14:48. A global failure mixed writer errors, false rejections, and broken judge replies. One sample excluded whole finalists. Repairs retained all three finalists and reported failures by task and language. Interview scoring retained at least five safe questions rather than discarding every set. Lesson: keep answer defects separate from measurement defects. See the [saved smoke replay](personal-career-benchmark.md#saved-smoke-replay).

4. Repeated honest-control stops, including 17:06. Invitations and bounded statements about missing evidence still triggered rejections. D39 first allowed one honest false reject, then three among 18. The repair retained perfect detection of valid planted defects and a separate fault ceiling. Lesson: publish the judge’s false-reject rate without calling it a writer invention rate. See the [verifier history](personal-career-benchmark-history.md#2026-11-16-verifier-calibration-repair) and [D39 amendments](../Planning/context/decisions.md#d39-amendment-provisional-benchmark-selection-and-heat-limit-2026-11-16).

5. Fixed two-of-three voting, November 16. Extra judgments reduced dependence on one rejection but exposed additional reply faults. Only rejected fields received repeat judgments. Lesson: a vote needs valid, independently recorded judgments and a fixed stopping rule. See the [voting report](../../master-cv-design-archive/ui-audit/final-t008-rejudge.md).

6. Truncation stop, 18:39. The recorded result showed 53 of 54 planted defects caught and five faulty probes. Replies exhausted their allowance before completing structured output. The repair used compact JSON, 16,384 total output tokens, and one 20,480-token retry. Reasoning used part of that allowance. Lesson: test actual response sizes and retain incomplete replies. See the [truncation repair](personal-career-benchmark-history.md#2026-11-16-truncation-and-cost-repair).

7. Cost correction, during the truncation repair. Reports added a purchase allowance and sometimes a held reservation to actual inference spend. The repair used provider charges and recorded reservations separately. Lesson: money reserved for a possible request is not money spent. See the same [cost evidence](personal-career-benchmark-history.md#2026-11-16-truncation-and-cost-repair).

8. Serial speed, November 16. Forecasts reached roughly 10–17 hours because requests waited for one another. The repair allowed 16 sessions and eight requests per provider, with a gradual increase and slower dispatch after throttling. Lesson: measure throughput and retry overhead before full approval. See the [concurrency report](../../master-cv-design-archive/ui-audit/final-t008-concurrency.md).

9. Whitespace runaway and duplicate IDs, November 17 at 04:33. Larger allowances still produced partial JSON followed by long whitespace. Another verdict repeated an identical absence ID. Repairs added whitespace stops and a fresh retry, and removed only identical repeated entries. Conflicting or missing IDs remained faults. D39 changed detection accounting to valid verdicts, with exhausted faults still under the 10% ceiling. Lesson: distinguish provider behavior from semantic judgment. See the [protocol repair](personal-career-benchmark-history.md#2026-11-17-campaign-043347-protocol-repair).

10. Voting fault exposure, 05:08. The old rule let a faulty extra judgment invalidate a valid majority. Each judgment now receives up to two fresh fault retries. Two valid agreeing judgments decide rejected fields. Offline replay recovered five probes but retained four unresolved faults among 72 controls. Lesson: a broken response supplies no vote. See the [current retry rule](personal-career-benchmark-history.md#2026-11-17-current-retry-and-vote-rule).

11. Final campaign billing stop, after the 05:55 start. One unknown $0.06615 reservation exceeded the old $0.05 threshold. The call-count threshold did not trigger. Repairs settled billing before dispatch and used a dollar ceiling of $5 for this campaign. Resume preserved 64 completed rows and reused 11 paid writers. Lesson: simulate interrupted billing before paying. See the [billing recovery](personal-career-benchmark.md#current-billing-recovery-2026-11-17).

12. Held exposure and resume failure. Settlement left stale holds, and corrected files initially failed their pinned operational hashes. Repairs settled 15 holds and updated the permitted hash records separately from the scientific identity. Lesson: test the exact resume entry point, including its identity rules. See the [held-exposure repair](../../master-cv-design-archive/ui-audit/final-t008-held-exposure.md) and [successful replay](../../master-cv-design-archive/ui-audit/final-t008-held-exposure2.md).

13. Internet outage and negative settlement. Connection timeouts and unreachable-network errors interrupted the full run. Subtracting a $0.472722 hold from a charge produced a negative settlement. Repairs restored receipt-based charges and reported overruns separately. New requests still required room under the caps. Lesson: record money already spent accurately, then control future spending. See the [settlement report](../../master-cv-design-archive/ui-audit/final-t008-bound-exceeded.md) and [D39 billing amendment](../Planning/context/decisions.md#d39-amendment-billing-settlement-and-exact-full-resume-2026-11-17).

14. OpenRouter key total limit, during owner recovery. The owner reports a key-level limit as another interruption. The requested source reports do not independently establish its exact timing or account change. Credit failures preserve the campaign for owner recovery. Lesson: budget each key’s remaining allowance alongside account funds and cumulative payer caps. Do not treat a genuine spending limit as a model failure.

15. Full-run exit and missing evidence. The original report stopped with 11 incomplete translation sessions, empty rating records, and no full letter run. Reporting succeeded, but inference status remained incomplete. Scoped continuation reused paid translation answers, added 324 letters, and collected 648 Astra AI ratings. Lesson: a report file does not establish completion. See the [original decision](../../master-cv-design-archive/ui-audit/benchmark-decision-tasks-v1.md) and [completion procedure](personal-career-benchmark-history.md#2026-11-17-complete-the-tasks-v1-decision).

16. Shared production guard defects, found during smoke diagnosis and confirmed after full inference. Summary rules attached dates to unrelated titles and confused degree majors with responsibilities. Job-evidence rules confused absent CV evidence with inability. Interview rules rejected supported premises and negated statements. Letter rules misread application targets, approved documents, and paraphrases. Repairs changed shared production functions and replayed unchanged answers, recovering 329 safety acceptances without losing an earlier acceptance. Genuine citation, language, length, and invention failures remained. Lesson: restrict hand-written rules to what they can establish reliably. See the [low-acceptance diagnosis](../../master-cv-design-archive/ui-audit/low-acceptance-diagnosis.md), [letter diagnosis](../../master-cv-design-archive/ui-audit/cover-letter-diagnosis.md), and [shared repair](personal-career-benchmark-history.md#2026-11-17-shared-guard-repair).

17. Final decision and overfitting risk. The combined matrix selects Terra medium provisionally for two tasks and selects none for four. Rules were repaired after holdout inspection. That creates overfitting risk, even though writer bytes stayed unchanged. The report discloses that these cases were not unseen. Lesson: use fresh holdouts after freezing repairs, and retain independent human review before release claims. See the [final decision](../../master-cv-design-archive/ui-audit/benchmark-decision-tasks-v1-final.md).

## Appendix: next-run preparation and release boundary

Before another paid study, replay saved responses, interrupted votes, timeouts, unknown charges, and exact resume behavior offline. Include a simulated key-limit failure. Compare a small promptfoo pilot with the existing runner for dispatch, records, and reports. Keep product-specific evidence rules and owner approval outside that pilot’s assumed guarantees.

The [OpenRouter Saudi research](../../master-cv-design-archive/ui-audit/openrouter-saudi-research.md) addresses contracts and overseas processing separately. Its dated findings do not authorize real CV processing. Synthetic benchmark success does not clear deployment, payment, privacy, or real-data gates.
