## Status and authority

Evidence and decisions have different authority:

| Label         | Meaning                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| UC            | User-confirmed context or instruction from this conversation                                                                               |
| USER-SELECTED | Explicit user choice, used for the Moyasar provider decision; not a supplier contract, merchant activation or independently verified claim |
| OD            | Requirement or proposal in the original supplied draft; not automatically approved                                                         |
| REC           | Our recommended product decision; awaiting acceptance                                                                                      |
| OPEN          | Decision, evidence, or commercial condition still unresolved                                                                               |
| OBS           | Behavior observed in supplied code or a public source; the observation date and limits matter                                              |
| WORKING       | Latest agreed conversational direction, not proof of implementation or final owner/merchant/legal approval                                 |
| APPROVED      | An explicit decision by the relevant project owner, with its date recorded; D01 now records the confirmed personal-only initial MVP        |

**OWNER DECIDED 2026-11-04** records product direction only. It does not approve implementation, activation or release; A01-A16 and the listed company inputs still apply.

### Payment gateway selection — 2026-10-20

The user explicitly chose **Moyasar (ميسر)** for Master CV because they believe Saudi customers recognize it, and wish to use that familiarity in the payment experience and marketing. This is a **user-selected provider**, not verified comparative market leadership or proof of brand-driven conversion. Do not keep treating provider selection itself as open, or automatically reopen the Paymob/Tap comparison. Merchant eligibility, contract, acceptance, fees, supported payment methods, automated recurrence, settlement and activation are **not approved/verified by this decision**. Confirm any visible Moyasar logo, name or partnership statement is permitted and accurate; do not imply endorsement. Customer-facing trust is primarily demonstrated by transparent amounts and available methods such as mada and Apple Pay (only when actually activated). No prices, recurring policy or implementation has been approved merely by choosing a gateway. See [payment integration](technical/architecture.md#payments) and [commercial model](business/product.md#commercial-model).
