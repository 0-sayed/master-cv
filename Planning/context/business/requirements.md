## Confirmed personal MVP at a glance

The **personal-only boundary is confirmed**. The seven capabilities below explain the proposed personal workflow drawn from the original requirements; PDF-only launch, the Start allowance and the free-beta-before-paid sequence are settled in D04/D05/D18/D29. Launch templates and the Focus copy allowance still need agreement.

| Main feature           | What the person can do                                                                        | Requirement  |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Start a CV             | Upload an existing CV or enter information from scratch, then confirm the facts               | B-C02        |
| Edit and preview       | Organize experience, education, skills, and other relevant sections while seeing the document | B-C03        |
| Improve the wording    | Review AI suggestions, accept or reject them, and undo changes without inventing credentials  | B-C04        |
| Tailor to a job        | Paste a job advertisement and see relevant strengths, evidence, and unanswered requirements   | B-C06, B-C07 |
| Keep useful versions   | Save different CVs for different applications and recover previous work                       | B-C05        |

Accounts, privacy, reliable saving, and clear recovery from failures support all seven features. The personal user downloads the CV and applies through their chosen job platform; employer recruitment operations remain outside this MVP.

## Candidate workflow

1. **Start and understand the offer.** Explain what the service checks, what it cannot guarantee, what is free, and what requires payment before the user invests substantial effort.
2. **Enter or import facts.** Accept the agreed document formats or guided fields. Show extracted information for correction; flag unclear names, dates, employers, and numbers. A scanned or unsupported file must lead to an understandable recovery path.
3. **Build the career record.** Capture contact details, experience, education, skills, and optional projects, certifications, languages, volunteering, and links. Users control which facts appear in each CV.
4. **Improve selected content.** Present the original and proposed text with a brief reason. The user accepts, edits, rejects, or undoes changes. Missing facts trigger questions, not fabricated achievements.
5. **Tailor to a job.** Paste a job description, confirm the intended role, and inspect requirements alongside supporting CV evidence. Create a separate variant without overwriting the master record.
6. **Review language and layout.** Select Arabic or English, verify names and terminology, and inspect the final document. Interface language and CV language are separate choices.
7. **Save and export.** Show truthful saving status, preserve versions, and export the selected variant in the available formats. The document the user previews must correspond to the downloaded content.
8. **Return and reuse.** Restore earlier work, create another variant, or download a CV to apply through an external job platform. Submitting directly to a participating employer would belong to the future employer product, where later edits must not change the submitted snapshot.

Example clarification: “هل لديك نتيجة قابلة للقياس لهذا العمل؟ يمكنك تركها بدون رقم.” Example evidence finding: “إدارة الميزانية مطلوبة في الوظيفة، ولم نجد لها دليلًا في المعلومات التي أدخلتها.” Neither statement presumes the user lacks the skill.

## Employer workspace

**Future reference only:** this section and B-E01–B-E08 are outside the confirmed personal MVP and require a separately authorized scope.

The original draft combines self-service language (“create a page in minutes”) with a managed service (“request a company page”, custom pricing). **REC:** validate the employer offering with one pilot company before implementing broad self-service onboarding.

Its bounded journey is company setup, brand preview, job draft, publication, application, team review, outcome, and job closure. A usable employer release needs a job description, requirements, application form, CV attachment or selected variant, consent notice, reviewers, notes, and status history. The prototype's title/city/type form is insufficient.

Use real operational counts for jobs, total applications, new applications, and interview-stage applications. Averages of prototype match scores are not a valid dashboard measure. Application-form customization is an unresolved scope boundary: start with fixed core fields and a small set of agreed questions, not an assumed general-purpose form builder.

**REC:** support guest application with an uploaded CV and explicit submission acknowledgement; offer a candidate account afterward. **OPEN:** whether company policy requires verified applicant accounts. The application must preserve the version actually submitted. “Immutable” means it does not change through ordinary editing, not that lawful retention and deletion rules cease to apply.

Separate employment type from work arrangement. Proposed states are new, in review, interview, hired, rejected, and withdrawn. Close or archive jobs through an intentional flow rather than deleting all associated applications. Matching can assist reviewers, but automated rejection, unsupported personality inference, and decisions based on irrelevant personal attributes are outside the recommended scope.

### Usage contract

A CV slot is one base CV. Its language versions and job-tailored copies belong to that base; revisions never count toward any allowance.

| Plan  | Base CV slots | Copies included per base CV                                                                                     | AI operations                                                            |
| ----- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Start | 1             | 1 English/other-language version and 1 job-tailored copy                                                        | 5 once only; no replenishment                                            |
| Focus | 10            | **Proposed fixture:** up to 20 combined language versions and job-tailored copies; not production configuration | 100 each billing month; reset at the billing-month boundary, no rollover |

One AI operation is one successfully delivered, user-requested task, regardless of internal model calls. Failed, cancelled and automatically retried work costs nothing extra; terminal failure/cancellation costs zero. Show the operation cost and check base/copy/AI limits before running AI; reserve and settle on the server at most once.

After downgrade or failed renewal, every existing CV remains readable, editable and exportable as PDF. Only creation of a new base CV above the plan limit is blocked; existing versions and revisions remain usable. T005 owns free-plan enforcement, including race-safe creation and operation accounting; T010 only adds paid grants and their lifecycle. See D05/D18 and the [commercial fixtures](../design/design-system.md#illustrative-commercial-data--not-a-production-offer).

## Delivery phases — engineering dependencies

These phases establish dependencies, not a binding schedule or fixed-price estimate. Implement through focused changes to the full fork, not a greenfield app plus an extracted editor.

**P0 — Establish the fork and prove the risky path.** Pin and run the complete application with synthetic data. Inspect existing features/schemas, apply branding/configuration, reproduce Arabic/import/export problems, review permitted AI routes and test representative source → review → edit → export flows. Set up owner-controlled infrastructure after quote review. Define release/retention/recovery targets and resolve production processing before accepting real CVs.

**P1 — Personal beta (T013).** Email account, import or manual entry, reviewed edits, one job-tailored version, Arabic and English PDF; saving, privacy, active-data deletion and email recovery must work. T012 owns backup restore/deletion replay. Remain synthetic until company registration and applicable real-data gates pass; mobile, billing and S32-S34 are not beta prerequisites.

**P2 — Paid personal launch (T015).** Depends on T008, T010 and T013. T008 may explicitly withhold either career tool. T010 adds paid grants, S24-S27 and the pricing section under D05/D18; PDF stays free. Moyasar written rate card, merchant-method activation, A09 and approved tax/refund/grace/settlement policies remain required. Mobile T014 is independent.

**P3 — Separately approved employer product.** Add companies, memberships, vacancies, selected CV submission snapshots, reviewer permissions, notes/status history and company-scoped notifications. Existing personal tracking does not supply these permissions or workflows. Guest applications and configurable form scope require product decisions. Test RLS/company boundaries and withdrawal/retention before opening it.

New LinkedIn integrations, job-board access, autonomous applications, interviews, human services or institutional offerings require evidence and explicit scope. Upstream code availability is an opportunity, not authorization to enable external access. Consult [business release phases](#requirements-and-release-phases) before changing what users are promised.

## Truthfulness and ATS assessment

The AI must never invent employment, qualifications, certifications, achievements, dates, numbers, seniority, or proficiency. It may improve wording and organization while preserving meaning. Numbers are useful only when supplied or explicitly confirmed; a CV without numerical achievements is not automatically poor. Users should be able to identify why a new claim appeared.

Present three separate assessments:

| Assessment                   | What it means                                                                                  | What it must not imply                                              |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PDF readability              | Extraction of the actual exported PDF preserves included facts, headings and reading order     | Every destination parser supports Arabic or behaves identically     |
| Writing and content findings | Specific completeness, clarity, repetition or phrasing tips; optional approved semantic review | A universal hiring-quality measurement                              |
| Evidence for a selected job  | Deterministic term coverage against the supplied job; optional approved evidence review        | A hiring probability or proof the person lacks an unmentioned skill |

**WORKING D22:** show three separate results, with no universal ATS percentage or score badge. Only defined term-coverage counts/ratios may appear inside selected-job evidence, with matched/unmatched terms, denominator, normalization method and exact CV/job versions. This measures literal coverage, not hiring probability. Without a supplied job, show that result as not checked. Replace the original promise of passing systems or reaching a hiring manager. Destination Arabic parser support is unknown unless independently verified and must be stated. [F01 hero/FAQ; F05 §5]

Tailoring should preserve relevant strengths rather than stuffing keywords. English and Arabic synonyms may support an explained match, but the system must not add an unsupported skill to raise a score. Employer assistance follows the same evidence rules.

## Saudi bilingual UX and accessibility

**REC:** professional Arabic-first experience with equally usable English, mobile layouts, clear status messages, and restrained visual design. Use understandable Arabic rather than excessive technical language. Preserve correct mixed-direction display for emails, phone numbers, dates, URLs, job titles, and product names. Let users review translations and proper nouns; do not silently propagate wording changes across every language variant.

Support meaningful section choices for graduates and experienced users. Photos, national identity numbers, marital status, religion, and health information should not be required by default. Confirm any later field against a specific lawful business purpose.

Target WCAG 2.2 AA as a **project quality target**: keyboard access, visible focus, labels, useful errors, contrast, reflow/zoom, and progress that is not conveyed by color alone. Validate key candidate and employer tasks with assistive technology. This target is not a claim about every private Saudi service's legal obligations. [B-S15](#b-s15)

**Proposed bilingual contract:** complete `ar-SA` and `en-US` product interfaces, with UI locale independent of CV language; English product copy remains pending owner review and Arabic is authoritative for meaning. Apply the [copy-deck contract](../design/design-system.md#bilingual-copy-deck-contract-proposed), locale/numeric/search rules and [AR/EN core journey acceptance matrix](#aren-core-journey-acceptance-matrix-proposed). Include localized validation, transactional messages, public metadata and unavailable states; inherited locale availability is not shipped product coverage. These are planning obligations, not implementation or release approval.

Saudi DGA's national design system is an optional government-service reference, not a requirement to use government branding or green for a private product. The overview was available through indexed official content; a full guide audit was not completed. Working tokens, fonts, spacing, colors and component choices are recorded in [the design system](../design/design-system.md) against the native Figma reference. Final owner approval and application acceptance remain open; the prior native review is recorded separately. [B-S16](#b-s16)

### ATS readiness (working)

**WORKING D22, 2026-11-03:** B-C07 acceptance and the A04 extension below are proposed release checks, not completed tests or release approval. Existing A02/A03/A11/A13 gates remain.

B-C07 acceptance:

1. Present **PDF readability**, **writing and content findings**, and **evidence for a selected job** separately in S10/S12 and the existing editor check panel. Reuse structured rules, the 76-rule PDF engine, PDF.js actual-export deep check and deterministic job-term matching. No new ATS library, paid parser on every action or AI foundation.
2. Each issue gives rule/version, result dimension, **blocker / warning / tip**, what is wrong, why it matters, evidence/location and a safe fix or review action. A blocker needs demonstrated extraction/content failure or a known destination constraint; layout or wording advice alone is not a rejection verdict. Preview fixes, require user acceptance, support undo and regenerate/recheck. Advice alone must not disable downloads.
3. Independently show not checked, checking, complete, skipped/partial, stale and failed. Missing evidence, truncation and timeout never become a successful whole-file check. Bind reports to exact downloaded bytes, CV revision and checker version. Changes to CV/template/font/renderer/checker invalidate affected results; a job change invalidates its evidence result, not an unchanged PDF check. Offer retry/manual review on failure.
4. Recognize curated Arabic and English section headings. Check actual-PDF RTL order, role/date/bullet associations, broken glyphs/Unicode mappings and included critical facts against the selected confirmed variant. Handle Latin and both Arabic-Indic digit sets, year-only precision, labelled Hijri/Gregorian calendars, bilingual duplication and mixed-script skills. Comparison normalization never rewrites saved facts or silently converts calendars.

### Mobile authentication: B-C17/B-C18

**Later T014 increment, off at launch; owner decided 2026-11-04, implementation gated:** Phase 1 includes verified-mobile SMS reset and recovery codes in addition to mobile + password signup/login and settings. SMS-only sign-in stays Phase 2 off. Numerical OTP-01..OTP-12 proposals live in [Security](../technical/security.md#mobile-authentication-proposed-disabled-until-a14-a16).

1. The server accepts supported Saudi mobile formats and Arabic-Indic digit input, normalizes to `+9665xxxxxxxx`, rejects unsupported destinations and keeps the auth number separate from editable CV contacts. A code beginning with zero survives typing, paste and autofill.
2. Phase 1 creation requires explicit create intent, accepted terms, enabled sign-ups, a password satisfying existing policy and successful SMS number verification; email is optional and an email link does not gate this mobile sign-up. Use enumeration-safe pre-verification responses. Phase 1 sign-in accepts mobile or an actual linked email plus password, preserves required MFA and never creates/merges an unknown account. An SMS code alone cannot sign in or reset a password.
3. A cryptographically generated six-digit code expires no later than 300 seconds; a challenge is single-use and bound to the initiating browser/session, number, purpose and provider request. Three incorrect submissions exhaust a challenge. A new code cannot reset the cumulative failure budget. Concurrent redemption, resend and uniqueness races preserve one valid outcome.
4. Enforce the approved send/verify budgets on number, trusted IP and browser before any supplier request. Initial proposed budgets are the mobile-auth policy in security.md; implement them server-side with shared atomic state. Countdown display cannot bypass cooldown. Abuse-control failure disables SMS sends; other approved login methods remain available.
5. Password login requires every enrolled factor; signup verification never issues an SMS-only session. Verified-mobile SMS reset and S15 recovery-code redemption grant only a one-use S16 reset continuation, then S14 password changed; enrolled MFA remains required at next sign-in. Direct plugin endpoints cannot bypass purpose, rate or reset controls. SMS-only sign-in remains server-denied in Phase 2 off.
6. Settings use fresh independent account proof before mobile verification; keep the old binding on cancellation/failure. Changed/removed numbers cannot reset. Numbers not verified for 12 months (proposed) need re-verification with account proof before reset. Reset notifies all verified contacts and revokes all other sessions; phone-only accounts get 8 hashed single-use codes once with atomic redemption/regeneration invalidation. No self-service recovery without number/codes/email; no claims-only support bypass.
7. Deletion and full personal-data export require server-checked, purpose-specific fresh proof, valid for at most five minutes and consumed once. SMS to a new number is insufficient. Independent access and enrolled MFA remain required for sensitive changes; no silent support override. Passkey/TOTP enrollment may support approved access controls but is not evidence that a phone-only account can reset its password.
8. AR/EN desktop/mobile cover password capture, optional email, login/MFA, signup/settings verification, S15 mobile/recovery-code states, S38 password reset → S16 → S14 password changed, S39 recovery codes, persistent S28 add-email, Account recovery methods/remaining codes and honest S36 help. Accessible semantic OTP input supports manual entry/paste/autofill and isolated Latin digits in RTL. SMS-only sign-in designs remain Phase 2 off.
9. Privacy notices distinguish authentication from marketing and CV contact details. No secret, code, full destination or SMS content appears in ordinary logs/analytics. Supplier locations, retention/deletion and user-rights handling are approved before real-number tests.
10. Phase 1 remains disabled until A14-A16 evidence passes; its owner recovery direction is decided. Phase 2 SMS-only sign-in requires a separate owner risk decision and evidence. Simulator sends never reach suppliers; provider acceptance is not handset delivery or proof of account creation/reset. Recovery is not paywalled.

## Evaluation and release gates

Start with a small smoke set to remove obvious ingestion, Arabic and schema failures. Then assemble **30–50 representative, consented or suitably anonymized cases** for an initial pilot evaluation. This is an initial decision set, not statistical proof of broad performance.

Include Saudi administrative and technical roles, graduates and experienced candidates, Arabic, English and mixed language, native PDFs, DOCX, long documents and incomplete histories. Include scans to test the unsupported-file/manual-recovery path unless OCR is explicitly enabled. Pair suitable cases with real or representative job descriptions. Avoid treating de-identified names as sufficient anonymization.

Create human-reviewed expected facts and requirement-to-evidence mappings. Compare providers using the same inputs, schemas and rubric. Measure extraction accuracy, unsupported claims, correction burden, usefulness, Arabic quality, latency and total accepted-result cost. Calibrate any automated judge against human review; task-specific evaluation matters more than generic benchmark rank. [T-S32](../technical/ai.md#t-s32)
